People ask me what my hobby is. Singular. Like I'm supposed to have one tidy answer ready for the dinner party small talk. I don't. I have four, and they actively hate each other. One counts my calories, one couldn't care less about calories, one has me questioning the nature of reality, and one just wants me to stop flailing in the deep end. Welcome to the mess.

## Swimming

![Swimming](/assets/img/serial-hobbyist/swim.png)

In 2024 I looked at my life and realized my biggest personality trait was "does the same thing every single day." Meanwhile there was a fitness allowance at work just sitting there, judging me for not using it. So I signed up for a two-week beginner swimming course at 27 years old, an age at which most people have already decided whether they can swim. I could not. Turns out learning to swim as an adult is 10% technique and 90% aggressively swallowing pool water while pretending you meant to do that.

## Strength Training

![Strength training](/assets/img/serial-hobbyist/strength.png)

I'm a software engineer, which means my brain runs a marathon every day while my body sits in a chair filing a formal complaint of unemployment. Eventually that imbalance catches up with you — bad sleep, low-grade irritability, the general vibe of a phone stuck at 1% battery. I'd lifted on and off since I was younger, but a few years ago I made it a permanent fixture. Not chasing a six-pack, just trying to convince my body it's still invited to the party.

## Religion & Philosophy

![Religion and philosophy](/assets/img/serial-hobbyist/reading.png)

This one kicked off in my early teens, when I decided the appropriate response to being a confused 13-year-old was to try and solve consciousness. Bold move. I still read across different philosophical and religious traditions, not because I've cracked it — I very much have not — but because it's the best tool I've found for understanding myself and everyone I have to deal with on a daily basis.

## Food Lover

![Food](/assets/img/serial-hobbyist/pie.png)

I'm South Asian, so let's be honest — this was never going to be a hobby, it's closer to a religious obligation. I eat everything. Everything. My wife and I have turned "let's travel somewhere" into a thinly veiled excuse to eat our way through it, one plate at a time. This is the one hobby with zero overlap with strength training — no calorie counting, no discipline, no restraint — which is exactly why the six-pack remains a rumor rather than a reality. I've made my peace with it. The food has not.

So no, I don't have a hobby. I have four completely unrelated personalities sharing one body and a very confused calendar.
